About LuckySpin

LuckySpin is Nigeria's most exciting coin-based spin-and-win platform. Spin the wheel, win coins, and convert them into real Naira rewards.

How It Works

Fund your account (min ₦5,000), then spin the wheel. Each spin costs ₦50. Win coins credited to your balance. Withdraw coins to your bank — 1,000 coins = ₦100.

Claim Coins

After winning coins on the wheel, use the Claim Coins button to move all your winnings into your coin balance. The system automatically calculates the exact Naira value (1,000 coins = ₦100).

Stages

Start at Stage 1 for easier wins. Progress to higher stages for bigger prizes with tougher obstacles. Return to earlier stages anytime.

Wheel Segments

🟡 Coins — Win coins added to your balance.
✕ Fail — No coins this spin.
🔁 Retry — Free respin at no cost!
