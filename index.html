How to Play GoalRush 14

The GoalRush 14 Jackpot consists of predicting the results of 14 matches selected every week.

To take part, register and have at least ₦100 in your account. Correctly predict all 14 results to win the Super Jackpot!

Making selections: Choose Home, Draw, or Away for each match. Each combination costs ₦100. Multiple picks per match increase combinations and stake.

Deadline: Bets must be placed before 14:00 Saturday (Nigeria time). The countdown shows the time remaining.

Results: Published every Monday afternoon in the Previous Results tab.

GoalRush Pick: Auto-selects one random outcome per match at ₦100 stake. You can add more selections after.

Prizes: 14 correct = Super Jackpot. 13 correct = 2nd Prize. 12 correct = 3rd Prize.
